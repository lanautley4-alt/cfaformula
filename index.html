Help & Sync

Syncing Between Devices

Your formulas are saved in your browser's local storage, so each device keeps its own copy. Use Export & Import to keep them in sync.

1
Export from your most up-to-date device

Click the button below. A .json file will download to your device.

2
Move the file to your other device

Use AirDrop, save to iCloud Drive, or email it to yourself — whichever is easiest.

3
Import on the other device

Open the app on your other device, come back to this Help screen, and click Import. Choose Merge to add new formulas without losing existing ones, or Replace to fully overwrite.
